NEON CLASH

2D ARCADE SHOOTER

Local 1v1 • Keyboard only

INSTRUCTIONS

Single Player – Arrow keys move, Space shoots. Survive 5 waves of Mini, Speed, Shield and Boss Bots. Avoid red energy fields.

Player 1 (blue) – W A S D move, F shoots.

Player 2 (red) – Arrow keys move, Enter shoots.

Walls and barriers block bullets – hide behind them! Press P or Esc to pause. First to defeat the opponent wins.
